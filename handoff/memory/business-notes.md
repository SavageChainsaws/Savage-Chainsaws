# Business notes — Savage Chainsaws

These are the facts about Jesse and the business gathered over this project's sessions. There's no separate persistent "memory" store outside this repo for this account as of 2026-10-30 — this file *is* the memory, kept in-repo on purpose so it travels with the project instead of being lost when a session ends.

## The business

- **Savage Chainsaws LLC** — a one-person mobile chainsaw and outdoor-power-equipment repair, rental, and build shop.
- Operates out of a **storage-unit facility** (rows of roll-up storage units, visible in brand photography — not a traditional storefront).
- Services both homeowners (first chainsaw, occasional use) and arborists/landscapers (daily-use workhorse equipment, repeat commercial customers).
- Known repeat commercial customer: **DeAngelo's Land Service** (rents multiple saws at once).
- Rental fleet includes STIHL chainsaws (e.g. MS194T, MS251, MS460) rented at $70-$90/day depending on model, $150 security deposit, damage liability capped per-unit.
- Also services/cross-references non-STIHL equipment — e.g. a **Country Line 25-ton log splitter** powered by a **Kohler SH265** engine (6.5 hp, 196 cc, GX200-class horizontal-shaft clone engine) came up as a real tune-up job.
- Uses a **Fischer trailer** setup and other red/black trailer-mounted equipment visible in shop photos.
